## Overview

An interactive quality control dashboard and analytical tool developed with **Streamlit** and **Python** to monitor, evaluate, and ensure the physicochemical and microbiological standards of yogurt production batches.

The application allows food safety specialists and quality control teams to track critical control points (CCPs), detect batch deviations in real time, and verify compliance with national and international dairy safety regulations.

---

## Key Features

* **Physicochemical Parameter Tracking:** Monitor pH, titratable acidity, total solids, fat content, and syneresis across production batches.
* **Microbiological Quality Monitoring:** Track lactic acid bacteria (LAB) viability and screen for contamination indicators (yeasts, molds, coliforms).
* **Automated Quality Thresholds:** Real-time visual alerts (Pass/Warning/Fail) based on preset food quality and safety limits.
* **Interactive Visualizations:** Trend charts, distribution histograms, and batch-to-batch consistency comparisons.
* **Exportable QC Reports:** Quick data export for audits, regulatory compliance, and ISO 22000 documentation.
